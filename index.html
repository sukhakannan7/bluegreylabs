Approach

The method.


A repeatable four-step process applied to every engagement — what separates a structured practice from ad-hoc advisory.

01

Diagnose the root cause

The presenting problem is rarely the real one. Every engagement starts with structured diagnosis — not assumptions.

02

Design for actual capability

Solutions fit the real technical capability of the people who'll use them. Google Forms outperforms enterprise software when it's actually adopted.

03

Ensure adoption

A system that isn't used has zero value. Adoption is a design constraint — not an afterthought.

04

Measure the outcome

Every intervention is tied to a quantifiable result. If you can't measure it, you don't know if it worked.

Guiding Principle

Adoption matters more than elegance. At one client, two sophisticated systems were rejected outright — engineers resisted the complexity. A phone-operable Google Forms solution succeeded where both failed. That lesson shaped every engagement since.

ACS Air Operations

Converting expert dependency into repeatable execution

+
8 → 2
Stock delays per project
₹30–40K
Savings per project

Context

Project-based AC contracting business. Nearly every routine decision ran through the founder — execution depended on his procurement judgment and on-site coordination.

Root Cause

Symptom: Material shortages, inconsistent execution. Reality: Expert knowledge was locked in the CEO's head. Dependency was structural — not a skills gap.

Intervention

A requirements checklist converting expert knowledge into a repeatable pre-project workflow. Plus a Google Forms resource analyser — phone-operable for non-technical site managers — tracking material allocation and predicting requirements. All computation automated.

What It Took

Two prior systems were scrapped. Engineers resisted passwords and operational overhead. The lesson: friction and usability determine whether a system exists in practice, not just on paper.

Dimension Friction and usability — the gap between a system that exists and one that gets used.

Context

B2B manufacturer of electrical chips and batteries. 2–3 year sales cycles, no online presence. Every client interaction depended on who was presenting that day.

Root Cause

Symptom: Inconsistent pitches, 2–3 days per prospect. Reality: No repeatable communication structure. Not a demand problem — a structure problem.

Intervention

Automated email pipeline delivering the full product catalog in website format — enabling prospects to self-qualify. Paired with a standardized pitch framework (ChatGPT skill) so every interaction follows a consistent structure.

Counterintuitive Result

Monthly prospects pitched dropped from 40–50 to 20–30. Conversion improved from 1-in-6 to 1-in-4. Filtering demand outperformed generating it.

Dimension Communication systems — and why filtering demand can matter more than generating it.
Triorra Looms Operations + Growth

From operator to strategist across three layers in one engagement

Context

Home-based clothing business, ~50 customers, stock across two locations. No formal metrics. Operations ran entirely on memory and intuition.

Layer 01 — Operations

Making it traceable

Lightweight ERP — Excel backend, Google Forms front end — for inventory, orders, and procurement. Automated the Instagram catalog pipeline with a GitHub-hosted site. Catalog handling: 3–4 hrs/week → sending one link.

Layer 02 — Behavioral

Making urgency work

Stock-clearance campaign. First attempt: positive reactions, few purchases — urgency was absent. Redesigned around scarcity + personalization via WhatsApp. ~8 orders in 20 days → ~25 items in 3 days. Profit: ₹2,000/20 days → ₹3,750/3 days.

Layer 03 — Strategy

Reframing the problem

Final sale day: ~12 prospects asking for sizes not in stock. Root cause: primary audience is 35+, so M/S sizes accumulate as dead stock — structural given wholesale procurement. An inventory problem was reframed as a segmentation problem. Solution: Instagram story-swap partnerships with brands whose audiences skew 20–35 — moving dead stock while building the missing demographic. In implementation.

Dimension Segmentation and inventory economics — the full arc from operator to strategist in a single engagement.
